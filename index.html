Founder & CEO

Building the infrastructure ambitious businesses deserve.

Sinethemba founded Eben Foundry on a simple observation: serious businesses in South Africa are routinely sold disconnected services — a website here, an ad campaign there — when what they actually need is a system. One connected engine, configured around how they earn.

Eben Foundry exists to make that engineering accessible: growth infrastructure that was once the privilege of large corporates, built deliberately for ambitious small and medium businesses — automation, marketing and commercial systems working as one machine.

The work is guided by building, systems thinking and economic participation: businesses that become visible, connected and economically actionable strengthen the communities around them. That is the point of the whole exercise.

"We don't sell digital services. We build the system a business grows on."

Where we are going

A long-term vision: businesses and communities that are visible, connected and economically actionable.

Eben Foundry's long-term direction is an economic opportunity network — a future where underserved businesses and communities can be discovered, engaged and transacted with as first-class participants in the economy, supported by the same growth infrastructure we build today.

That is a vision, not a current product. Today, the commercial priority is exactly what it says across this website: designing, building and operating growth systems for real businesses.

We state it plainly because direction matters — and because we would rather show you what exists than promise what doesn't.
